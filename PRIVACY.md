Privacy Policy
Tapo Auto Shutdown
Tapo Auto Shutdown does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the plugin author.

The plugin stores the Tapo P110 connection details entered by the user in OctoPrint's plugin settings. These details are used to communicate directly with the configured Tapo device on the user's local network.

Obico integration
If the Obico plugin is installed and configured, Tapo Auto Shutdown can communicate with the Obico service through the installed Obico OctoPrint plugin.

The plugin sends requests to enable or disable Obico AI monitoring for the connected printer. It does not independently collect or upload webcam images, print files, or other printer data.

Any data processed by Obico is subject to Obico's own privacy policy and terms.

For information about Obico's data handling, please refer to:

https://www.obico.io/blog/obico-privacy-data-security/

Changes
This privacy policy may be updated when the plugin's functionality changes.
